Copilot card
ZAVA COMP RULES (paste before any comp question)
1. Data: Comp_Detail is cafe x fiscal week x daypart x channel. Use only rows that have a Cafe ID. Rows named "<Region> Total" or "System Total" are subtotals: never add them.
2. Comp population, evaluated separately for every week (including base weeks): Open Date on or before the date 18 months before that week starts (Week 45: 2025-05-02; Week 44: 2025-04-26) AND that week's Cafe Status = Open AND Cafe Status LY = Open.
3. Comp sales % = sum Net Sales / sum Net Sales LY - 1. Traffic % = sum Transactions / sum Transactions LY - 1. Check % = (Net Sales / Transactions) / (Net Sales LY / Transactions LY) - 1. Never average cafe percentages.
4. Comp bridge: traffic pts = Traffic %; check pts = (Check - Check LY) x Transactions / Net Sales LY. They add up to comp sales %.
5. Fiscal weeks run Monday-Sunday; FY2026 Week 1 began 2025-12-29. Holidays that move weeks (Labor Day: LY Week 36, TY Week 37) must be compared holiday to holiday.
6. Not in the data: catering split, tender type, new vs repeat guests, guest counts. Say "not available in the dataset". Do not estimate or use proxies.
7. Style: comp sales, then traffic, then check; one decimal; contributions in pts; always state the week, the comp cafe count and the filter used.

Glossary

Comp sales (SSS)Same-cafe sales growth vs the same fiscal week last year, on the comp population.
Comp populationCafes open 18+ months and not temporarily closed in the week this year or last year.
Traffic / checkTransactions (orders, not guests) and net sales per transaction. (1 + comp) = (1 + traffic) x (1 + check).
ptsPercentage points of last year's sales; contributions in pts add up to comp.
Channel mix vs within-channelCheck can rise because orders moved to a higher-check channel (mix) or because each channel's check rose (rate).
Base periodThe four weeks before the reported week, combined.
Comparison groupCafes or regions that didn't get an event; their change stands in for "what would have happened".
ResidualThe part of a bridge no driver explains. Show it; never hide it. With as many effects as groups it's zero by construction, so it isn't a test.
Observed / Estimate: strong / Estimate: weakThe three tags for every number: recomputable from the data; a modeled effect clearly outside the placebo range with supporting evidence; any other modeled effect.
Placebo testThe same method run on weeks where nothing happened. Whatever it finds there is noise or bias.
LapThe comparison against last year's event in the same week (here, last year's free-pastry week).
1P / 3PFirst-party digital (Zava app and web) / third-party delivery marketplaces.
Certified dataset / semantic modelThe governed Power BI model behind the reports; the source of truth for tie-outs.
PTDPeriod-to-date: P11 so far = Weeks 44-45.
