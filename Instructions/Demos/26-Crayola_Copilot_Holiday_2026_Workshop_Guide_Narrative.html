Supplemental hands-on lab

Polish the launch chart in Excel

Out-of-the-box skillFile must be in cloud storage

What we are doing

Invoke the workbook's chart-design skill on the raw monthly series in the A tab, then compare it with the B tab output.

Why it matters to Crayola

A launch audience reads a polished trend chart faster than a raw grid. This skill turns launch performance into a deck-ready visual in under a minute.

⇩

Open this exact file: Excel Plugins.xlsx. Prerequisite: save it to OneDrive or SharePoint before opening it in Excel. A local-only file will not work for this lab.

  1. Open Excel Plugins.xlsx from OneDrive or SharePoint.
  2. Open 05a Chart Design DATA and select cells A5:D17.
  3. Open Copilot in Excel and ask it to use the chart-design skill on the selected range.
  4. Paste the prompt below and run it. Keep the workbook open while the skill works.
  5. Compare the result with 05b Chart Design DEMO, the paired expected-output tab.
Exact Excel invocation
Use the chart-design skill on the selected range in "05a Chart Design DATA." Show monthly Revenue ($000s) as a line and Gross Margin % on a secondary axis. Use one purposeful color per series, horizontal gridlines only, a bottom legend, clean number formats, and a takeaway title that states the change from January to December. Make the result ready to use in the Crayola Holiday 2026 launch deck.
Before13 rows of monthly revenue, margin, and new-customer data.
AfterA dual-axis trend chart with restrained formatting and a takeaway title.
Visible valueThe paired output computes: revenue grew 94% while gross margin expanded 510 bps.

Why this skill

chart-design reads naturally for a consumer-products launch, produces a result the room can judge immediately, and feeds the PowerPoint hero moment. The workbook also includes clean-data, but chart design makes the value more visible in under a minute.

What good looks like

A clean monthly line chart; revenue and margin use separate axes; colors encode the two series rather than decorate; labels are not crowded; and the title states the business takeaway instead of saying only “Revenue and Margin.”

Personalize this: replace the sample monthly series with your product, account, or channel KPIs while preserving the same four-column layout.

30-second fallback

Open 05b Chart Design DEMO. Point to the design-decision table and cells B16:B18, then say: “The skill chooses the visual treatment and computes the takeaway; changing the A-tab data updates the B-tab output.”

Facilitator only | fallbacks, rough edges, and preview language

Pre-stage the hero

Seed two prior launch decks, the sales workbook, and the Crayola template in Marketing SharePoint. Rehearse modules 3 → 4 → 8. Keep a screenshot of the generated deck if Work IQ retrieval is slow.

Known rough edges

Researcher can take several minutes. Generated PowerPoint layouts still need human review. App Builder is for a simple internal Lists-backed tracker, not a customer-facing production app. Pre-run Cowork and keep the completed package open.

If availability varies

Say: “This capability is not enabled in every tenant. We will use the prepared output so you can evaluate the workflow, then confirm licensing, region, and rollout status with your Microsoft administrator.” Do not imply a date or GA status not present in the supplied materials.

Researcher guardrail

Researcher measures category visibility and recommends content actions. It does not increase citation or ranking by itself; the lift comes from content Crayola chooses to publish.

Agent guardrail

Keep it read-only. Require confirmation before any action that changes, sends, shares, or approves content. Ask attendees to verify citations and escalate when the source folder does not answer.

Excel source note

The supplied workbook names chart-design and shows paired A/B tabs, but it does not document a special command token. Use the Copilot pane and the full natural-language invocation in this guide.
